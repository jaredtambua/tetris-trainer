# TETR.IO ruleset specification

This document is the future specification for the authoritative engine. A
mechanic may move beyond **TO VERIFY** only after its behavior has been checked
against a reliable source and the source and decision have been recorded here.
Unresolved behavior must not be implemented by assumption.
